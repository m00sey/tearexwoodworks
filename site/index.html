How it works

Photo in, carving out

Four steps from your first message to a finished piece on the wall.

  1. Send the idea

    A photo, a logo, a pet, a rough sketch. Tell us the size you’re thinking and where it will hang.

  2. Model and preview

    The image gets turned into a 3D relief. You see a preview and a quote before anything gets cut.

  3. Carve

    The CNC roughs out the panel, then details it with fine bits. Deep pieces run for hours.

  4. Finish and hang

    Hand sanding, cleanup, oil or finish, and hanging hardware. Pick up locally or we ship.

Turnaround depends on size and depth. Ask when you get in touch and you’ll get a straight answer.

The workshop

A small shop with a big saw blade

Tea Rex Woodworks is a one-shop operation in southeastern Pennsylvania. The CNC does the heavy lifting on relief and lettering. Everything else, from picking the board to the last coat of oil, is done by hand.

Solid hardwood, carved from single boards or glued-up blanks so the grain runs through the piece and it ages like furniture.
