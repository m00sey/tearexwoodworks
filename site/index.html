How it works

Photo in, carving out

Three stages from your idea to a finished piece, shown here with Rico: the photo, the design, the carving.

  1. Input

    You provide the concept, or we work from a photo.

  2. Design

    We flip your idea into a design. Once you approve it, it is turned into a 3D model for the CNC router.

  3. Production

    Let the wood chips fly! Once the carving is done we sand it and apply the finish, usually 100% pure tung oil and a protective layer of wax.

Turnaround depends on size and depth. Ask when you get in touch and you’ll get a straight answer.

The workshop

A small shop with a big saw blade

Tea Rex Woodworks is a one-shop operation in southeastern Pennsylvania. The CNC does the heavy lifting on relief and lettering. Everything else, from picking the board to the last coat of oil, is done by hand.

Solid hardwood, carved from single boards or glued-up blanks so the grain runs through the piece and it ages like furniture.

  • Solid hardwoodCherry, walnut, mahogany, oak and exotics on request
  • Plaques to panelsFrom small plaques up to the size of the CNC bed
  • Pickup or shipLocal pickup near Philadelphia, or shipped crated
